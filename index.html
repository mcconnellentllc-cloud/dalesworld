About Dozers Games

Founded and operated by Dale Chaney, Dozers Games brings the ULTIMATE mobile video game experience right to your doorstep. Based in the Heart City of Valentine, Nebraska and Holyoke, Colorado, we serve events across Nebraska, Colorado, Wyoming, and South Dakota.

Our luxury, limo-style theater on wheels features 7 widescreen high-definition TVs, surround sound, and multiplayer gaming heaven. Up to 28 players can play at the same time with room for more friends on stadium seating.

From school post-proms to county fairs, birthday parties to corporate events — if you're looking for entertainment that everyone will remember, Dozers Games delivers.
