The Dog Behind the Name

Every great story has a heart — and the heart of DalesWorld and Dozers Games is a four-legged hero named Dozer. Before the mobile video game trailers, before the Archery Tag arenas and county fair appearances, there was a bond forged between a man and his K9 partner on the roads and open rangeland of Cherry County, Nebraska.

Dozer served alongside Dale Chaney with the Cherry County Sheriff's Department in Valentine, Nebraska — the Heart City, nestled in the Sandhills of the nation's largest county by area. Together, they patrolled over 6,000 square miles of rugged Nebraska terrain, serving and protecting the communities of Cherry County.

As a law enforcement K9, Dozer was more than a partner — he was family. Through long shifts, harsh Nebraska winters, and the unpredictable demands of rural law enforcement, Dozer was always there: alert, loyal, and ready. He was the kind of partner who never let you down and never asked for anything but your trust in return.

His Legacy Lives On

When Dale Chaney founded Dozers Games, the name was never a question. Everything he built — the mobile video game trailer, the entertainment company that brings joy to kids and families across Nebraska, Colorado, Wyoming, and South Dakota — carries Dozer's name and spirit.

From post-prom events keeping teenagers safe, to birthday parties that light up kids' faces, to county fairs and community celebrations — Dozer's legacy is one of service, loyalty, and bringing people together. The same qualities that made him an extraordinary K9 partner now fuel a business dedicated to creating unforgettable experiences.

Dozers Games isn't just a company. It's a tribute to a partner who gave everything. Every event, every smile, every game played in that luxury trailer on wheels — that's Dozer's legacy rolling forward.
